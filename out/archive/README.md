# Archived pre-launch projections

These were generated during development, days before their deadline, to exercise
the pipeline. They are NOT part of the public accuracy record and must never be
scored or published as predictions.

The committed methodology is that a projection is built from a snapshot taken
shortly before its deadline, because team news lands in the final hours. These
were built up to 149h early. Scoring them would both understate the model and
misrepresent the method.

They are kept rather than deleted: they are evidence of how the pipeline was
built, and deleting things from a directory whose whole purpose is an auditable
record is a habit worth not starting.
