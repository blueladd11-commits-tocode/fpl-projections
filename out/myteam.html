How this works
Swap players
Tap a starter, then a substitute. We stop any swap the game would not allow.
Captain
Right-click a player (or press C) to give him the armband and double his score. V for vice-captain.
Drafts
Keep up to six different teams and flick between them — each tab shows what that team should score.
Stuck?
Hit Build me a squad and we pick a full fifteen inside the budget for you.
Why are these numbers smaller than other sites’?
Because ours are what actually happens. Last season exactly one player in the league averaged over 6.3 points a week — sites showing you half a squad at that level are flattering you. Ours are checked against reality every week, in public, on the accuracy record.
